# Shepherd University
# https://www.shepherd.edu/personal-statement-writing-prompts/

A list of writing prompts is provided below. All personal statements should be a minimum of 250
words in length and can be submitted via the application portal.

Have you faced a challenge or failure? How has that affected you? How did you learn and grow from
the experience?

We’re looking for students who will become positive additions to our student body. If this describes
you, share how you’ll become involved at Shepherd, and how your background, identity, interests, or
talents will add to our well-rounded student community.

Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization, sparking a period of personal growth and a new
understanding of yourself or others.

Describe an engaging topic, idea, or concept of personal importance to you. What interests you about
this, and where do you turn to learn more?

If these writing prompts aren’t resonating with you, students are welcome to write an essay on the
topic of their choice. However, we ask that the topic remain relevant and appropriate for the
college admission process.
