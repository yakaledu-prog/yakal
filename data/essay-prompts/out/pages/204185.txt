# University of Mount Union
# https://www.mountunion.edu/admission/application-essay-instructions

As a general guideline, the length of your application essay should be a minimum of 300 words.

Please respond to one of the following questions.

What would you like our admission committee to know about you that we cannot learn simply by
reviewing your high school transcript and other records submitted in support of your application?

What significant leadership or volunteer roles have you played? How have they impacted your life?

What values do you cherish the most and how have you demonstrated them through the choices that you
have made?

*If applying to the Bachelor in Nursing program, please instead respond to the essay prompt for
Nursing Program Applications listed below.

As a general guideline, the length of your application essay should be a minimum of 300 words.
Explain the reason for leaving your prior institution and the reason for selecting University of
Mount Union.

*If applying to the Bachelor of Science in Nursing program, please instead respond to the essay
prompt for Nursing Program Applicants listed below.

Please write a three paragraph essay describing your educational goals.

*If applying to the Bachelor of Science in Nursing program, please instead respond to the essay
prompt for Nursing Program Applicants listed below

You are not required to respond to the prompts above. An essay in response to the following question
will be used in review of both your Mount Union application and the nursing program application.

Your application essay must be at least one page and a minimum of 500 words in length. Please
describe why you are interested in nursing as a major and why you want to enroll in the Bachelor of
Science in Nursing program at Mount Union.
