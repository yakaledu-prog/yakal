# Barnard College
# https://barnard.edu/barnard-short-answer-questions

In addition to the personal essay which you will submit through the Common Application or
QuestBridge Application, Barnard asks first-year applicants to respond to institution-specific short
answer questions. Together with your application materials, your responses to these questions help
us gain a deeper understanding of who you are.

Below are the 2026-2027 questions for first-year students:

Required : At Barnard, inquiry starts with asking bold questions about the world. What is one bold
question on your mind and what about it piques your curiosity? (50 words maximum)

Required : Barnard College is shaped by trailblazing women with a multitude of perspectives. Think
of a woman - historical, fictional, contemporary, or personally significant - whose views or
approach to problem-solving differ from your own, but who excels at bringing people together. What
lesson from her approach would shape how you show up at Barnard both inside and outside of the
classroom? (200-250 words)
