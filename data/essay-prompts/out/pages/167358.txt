# Northeastern University
# https://admissions.northeastern.edu/how-to-apply/first-year-applicants/

If you choose to submit standardized testing, you may submit the SAT(CEEB Code 3667), ACT (College
Code 1880), or both. Northeastern super-scores and will only consider your best score between the
two types of tests.

All applicants from U.S. and international high schools will be able to choose to submit
standardized testing (SAT or ACT) or not.

Students applying to the College of Arts, Media and Design can submit a portfolio under their
Application Status Check after applying to Northeastern. Applicants are encouraged to complete all
forms, including Academic Profile, Media Uploads, and Personal Statement (500-word limit). Please
only submit one portfolio. Additional submissions will not be reviewed. Click here for more
information and deadlines.
