Short Answer * Please answer the below prompt in 300-400 words for consideration into the Honors
College. The submitted response will be treated as your writing sample and evaluated for spelling,
grammar, etc. as part of the admissions process. We recommend composing your response and checking
it in a text editor first for refinement before pasting it in the application for submission. There
will be many opportunities to tell the Honors College faculty about your achievements. This isn’t
one of them. Every so often a person turns out to be wrong about something they were sure of. Tell
us about one of yours in 300 to 400 words. What did you believe, and what was false about it? It
doesn’t have to be a large belief. The small ones are often better, and we’d rather read something
true than something impressive. Then tell us where the mistake actually was. Sometimes the
information is bad and you reason from it perfectly well. Other times the information is fine and
the thinking is what breaks down. Which was it for you? Take us to the moment you found out. Who was
there and what got said. If part of it doesn’t flatter you, leave that in. You don’t need to tell us
the lesson. Tell us instead whether you think you’d catch it next time, and how. Be honest: most
people say yes. Is it true?
